ABOUT US

Who We Are

A Team Built Around Technology & Innovation


Happy Sad Mi is a collaborative team of IT professionals passionate about solving real-world problems through software, web technologies, automation, and data-driven solutions.


The name "HappySadMi" has a special story behind it. In our native language, the phrase is a playful expression that roughly means "we're happy too." At the same time, when read in English, it amusingly combines the words "Happy" and "Sad" in a single name a contrast that often makes people smile and sparks curiosity. We embraced that uniqueness because it reflects our personality as a team: we believe that even during challenging moments, there's always room for creativity, humor, and optimism while building meaningful technology.


Our journey began in college, where we met as classmates and eventually worked together on our thesis project. Through countless hours of planning, development, testing, and problem-solving, we built not only a successful project but also a strong foundation of teamwork, communication, and collaboration. That shared experience taught us how to tackle complex challenges, adapt under pressure, and deliver solutions as a unified team.


We combine creativity, technical expertise, and analytical thinking to help businesses improve efficiency and build impactful digital products.
